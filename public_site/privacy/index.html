Tame Your Biz

Privacy policy

Effective September 15, 2026. Tame Your Biz is operated by Robbie Jenkins. For privacy questions or account assistance, contact tameyourbiz@gmail.com.

Scope and development status

This policy describes this introductory website and the current development version of Tame Your Biz. The app is not publicly released. Company cloud backup, company sharing, and Google Drive job photo storage are not enabled in the current version. We will update this policy before introducing services that change how information is handled.

Records on your device

The app stores the business information you enter locally, including customer contact details, work records, estimates, invoices, expenses, attachments, employee records, and vehicle and mileage information where those features are used. Local business records are not uploaded merely because you create a sign-in account. Access by other people who can use your device is also affected by your device security and app configuration.

Optional online account

If you choose the email and password account feature, Google Firebase Authentication processes your email address, authentication credentials, account identifier, and technical information needed to provide and protect authentication. Account information is used for sign-in, password recovery, email verification, and account management. It does not enable company backup in the current version.

Google explains its handling of information in its Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy documentation.

Photos and device permissions

When you choose to capture or attach an image, the app uses the relevant camera or file selection facilities. Photos may contain customer or other personal information; only add information you are authorized to keep. Device permissions can be managed through system settings. A selected attachment and an original image in your photo library can be separate copies.

Website visits and support

This introductory website has no advertising, analytics scripts, account forms, or tracking cookies added by us. Hosting providers may process IP addresses, request details, and security logs to deliver and protect the website. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include and use them to respond and resolve the request. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary customer records.

Sharing and purposes

Information is used to provide the features you choose, respond to support requests, and protect the service. We do not sell personal information or use business records for targeted advertising. Service providers process information needed for hosting, authentication, and email. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or necessary to investigate misuse or protect rights and safety.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until removed through applicable app or device controls. Copies you export or save elsewhere must be managed separately. In the current account-only version, deleting your sign-in account removes the Firebase Authentication account but does not erase local business records. Contact us for assistance with account or privacy requests. Support correspondence is kept as needed to address the request and related operational or legal obligations; provider security and backup retention may differ.

Your choices and security

You can use local features without creating an online account. You can manage device permissions, delete the optional sign-in account, and contact us about access, correction, or deletion of information we hold. Available rights may depend on your location. No storage or transmission system is guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device protected and maintain appropriate backups of important records.

Future storage options

Google Drive job photo storage and optional app cloud backup are planned, not currently connected. If introduced, Drive access will require your authorization and will be limited to relevant files. Connecting Drive will not be required for local use. Storage choices, data handling, and applicable charges will be explained before you enable those services.

Children and changes

Tame Your Biz is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information to us. Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated effective date; material changes will be communicated as appropriate.
